Why it matters

The cost signal is in the evidence — before it reaches your claims

Behind every number is a woman navigating work, family and symptoms at the same time — often without ever mentioning it to her employer or her insurer.

Prevention over treatment

The strongest women's health research increasingly points upstream — to what can be prevented, delayed or better managed. Reading it early is what turns evidence into a prevention programme rather than a claims line further down the road.

Underwriting that reflects reality

Guidelines lag the literature. When your actuarial and medical teams work from current, appraised evidence rather than dated assumptions, your view of risk tracks the real picture more closely.

Products members actually want

A whole generation of women is underserved in menopause and midlife health. The evidence points to where credible, differentiated cover and support can be built — ahead of the market.

How the intelligence is built

Graded evidence — nothing oversold

From the primary literature

We monitor the major peer-reviewed databases and journals in menopause, ageing and women's health every month.

Critically appraised

Each study is assessed for design, sample size and bias risk. Strong evidence, emerging signals and speculation are labelled as exactly that.

Fully referenced

Every claim links back to its original study, so your own actuarial and medical teams can verify it and go deeper.

Independent by policy

No pharma, supplement or device funding, and no product of our own to sell. The evidence is the only thing we represent.

Distilled for decisions

Written for busy teams: a clear ten-minute read that surfaces cost, risk and prevention implications without the noise.

Corrected in the open

When new evidence changes a conclusion, we say so visibly in the next brief — never quietly.
