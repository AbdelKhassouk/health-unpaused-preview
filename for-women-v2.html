Good to know

Questions, answered honestly

Am I losing my mind? +
No — even when it genuinely feels that way. Brain fog is one of the most common things women describe in perimenopause and menopause: words vanish mid-sentence, names go missing, focus slips. It's real, it has a biological basis, and in the overwhelming majority of cases it is not dementia. Understanding what's happening helps in itself — and if you're worried, that's exactly the conversation to take to your own doctor. What we share is education, not medical advice.
What does it cost? +
Your first 7 days are free. After that it's €13/month with no commitment — you can cancel anytime, directly in Skool.
Where does the community live? +
On Skool — a calm, ad-free community platform. It works in your browser and there's a Skool app for your phone. Joining takes a couple of clicks: skool.com/women-unpaused ↗
Is this medical advice? +
No. Everything we share is educational information with full sources — built to make your conversations with your own doctor better, never to replace them.
Who actually reads all the research? +
Nicky Draper — 18 years as a medical researcher in ageing, longevity and women's health, and a former nurse with 20 years in primary and acute care. Every claim in every brief is traceable to its source. Read more on our Research & Evidence page.
I'm in perimenopause / post-menopause — is it still for me? +
Yes. Peri, meno, post — and anyone supporting a partner through it. The research we cover spans the whole journey, and the community is open to wherever you are in yours.
Will you try to sell me supplements? +
Never. We're independent — no pharma funding, no affiliate products, nothing to push. We sell knowledge, and that's the whole business model.
