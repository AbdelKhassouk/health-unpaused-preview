Good to know

Questions, answered honestly

Is the brief free? +
Signing up for the brief is free. The women's community on Skool is free for 7 days, then €13/month with no commitment. Doctors, HR and insurers: we'll follow up personally about your subscription.
Am I losing my mind? +
Many women describe brain fog — lost words, walking into rooms and forgetting why. It's common, it's real, and in the overwhelming majority of cases it is not dementia. Understanding it is the first step. (Educational information, not medical advice.)
Is this medical advice? +
No. Everything we share is educational information with full sources — built to make conversations with your own doctor better, never to replace them.
Will you try to sell me supplements? +
Never. We're independent — no pharma funding, no affiliate products. We sell knowledge, and that's the whole business model.
