For Doctors

The menopause evidence, appraised — in the time of one consultation

Many of the women in your consulting room describe brain fog, exhaustion, anxiety, low mood and broken sleep — often before anyone has said the word menopause. You want to help. A ten-minute appointment, and an evidence base that moves faster than the guidance, make that hard.

Our monthly clinician brief gives you the current evidence, critically appraised and fully referenced — written by researchers, for clinicians.

What's in the clinician brief

  • Key new studies in menopause and women's health, appraised for quality and clinical relevance
  • Where new evidence diverges from current guidance (for example, NICE) — flagged clearly, never overstated
  • Plain-language patient explanations you can reuse in consultations
  • Full reference lists — every claim traceable to its source
  • A ten-minute read, distilled from 100+ pages of new research each month

Our briefs are educational and support your clinical judgement — they are not a treatment protocol, and never replace your own reading of the primary literature.

Source transparency

Nothing to take on trust

A brief is only as good as the sources behind it.

Every conclusion we hand you is traceable back to the primary literature, so you can appraise it the way you would appraise any paper before it informs a consultation. Where the evidence appears to have moved ahead of current guidance, we note it — and leave the clinical judgement where it belongs: with you.

Every claim referenced

Each statement in the brief links to its original study. The reference list is part of the product, not an afterthought — go to the source whenever you want to.

Evidence graded honestly

We distinguish clearly between strong evidence, emerging signals and speculation. Weak evidence is labelled as weak — never dressed up as certainty.

Independent of industry

We accept no funding from pharmaceutical, supplement or device companies. Our revenue comes from subscribers, so our only incentive is to be right.
