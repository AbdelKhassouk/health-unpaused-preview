Menopause affects half the population and sits at the intersection of healthcare, workforce retention and insurance economics. Health Unpaused monetises one evidence base across four paying segments — with a platform built to expand into adjacent verticals.

The model

One research base. Four ways to monetise it.

Four revenue streams

Women (community membership), doctors, HR teams and insurers — each subscribing to a tailored briefing built from the same underlying research work.

Low marginal cost

The research is done once per month; every additional subscriber in every segment is near-pure margin. The backend is built for automation and scale.

Built to be trusted

Independence is the moat: no pharma, supplement or device funding, every claim graded and sourced. In a market full of product-pushing, an evidence-only voice is hard to copy.

Why now

The timing works in our favour

Regulatory tailwind

Workplace menopause policies are moving from nice-to-have to expected across Europe. HR budgets are following.

Research velocity

The volume of new menopause research is accelerating — which makes curation more valuable every month, not less.

Trust vacuum

The consumer space is saturated with product-pushing. An independent, evidence-only voice is a defensible position.

The product

A product people make time for.

Ten minutes, once a month, distilled from 100+ pages of new research. Respect for the reader's time is the product — and the same discipline carries across all four briefings.

Where it can go next

Built for expansion — future phases

The name "Health Unpaused" was chosen deliberately so the platform can serve more than menopause. These are directions we're building toward, not products we sell today — each is a future phase, to be validated and resourced in turn.

Future phase

Young women

Cycle knowledge and hormonal health for younger audiences — the same evidence-first approach, earlier in life.

Future phase

Women & performance

The research on hormones, training and physical performance, distilled for women who want to train and live with the science.

Future phase

Men & andropause

"(Wo)Men Unpaused" — extending the same rigorous method to testosterone decline and men's mid-life health.

Future phase

Curated commerce

A webshop and affiliate layer of independently vetted products — added only where the evidence supports them, without compromising our independence.

These expansion phases describe the platform's direction, not live products. No timelines or revenue are implied.
