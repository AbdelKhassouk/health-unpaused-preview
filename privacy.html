Privacy notice

How we handle your email

Short version: we only use your email to send you the briefing you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What we collect

Your email address and which briefing you chose (woman, doctor, HR or health insurer). Nothing else is required.

Why

To send you the monthly Health Unpaused brief and, if you chose the women's briefing, the link to our community. We do not sell or share your details with third parties for their own marketing.

Who processes it

Our email is sent through Klaviyo, our email service provider, which stores your address securely on our behalf. Our community for women runs on Skool, which has its own terms and privacy policy when you join there.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your details at any time by writing to hello@healthunpaused.com.

Not medical advice

Everything we send is educational information with sources. It is not medical advice and never a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
